Xianxia-themed content mod for Terraria/tModLoader.

v0.1.0 - Core cultivation system with 9 realms, spiritual energy,
tribulation (Minor + Heart Demon), 12 bosses with unique AI, 17 enemies
with custom behaviors, 8 biomes with ambient effects, 5 town NPCs with
shops/commissions, pill quality RNG, expert/master scaling, en-US/zh-Hans.
