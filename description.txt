Xianxia-themed content mod for Terraria/tModLoader.

Development version 0.1.0. Includes 9 cultivation realms, 4 tribulation types,
12 bosses, 17 ordinary enemies, 8 biomes, 5 friendly NPCs and 9 crafting stations.
Spirit energy, pills and quality, inscriptions, weapon refinement, summon contracts,
commissions, fishing crates and an optional Spirit Tide Night event are implemented.
Supports en-US and zh-Hans. Boss Checklist integration is optional.

Debug drops are disabled by default. Permanent growth multiplier defaults to 1.0.
Native build and dedicated-server content loading are checked automatically.
Full playthrough, real multiplayer, combat balance and final artwork remain under validation.
Calamity is optional; dedicated Calamity balance/progression adaptation is not implemented.
